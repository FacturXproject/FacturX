F12 — Human Correction Form: Validation Rules

1. Purpose

The human correction form allows users to review and correct fields extracted from a PDF invoice before the invoice is used by later processing steps.

2. Required fields

The following fields must not be empty when saving a draft:

Invoice number

Invoice date

Seller name

Buyer name

Each invoice line must also have a description.

3. Numeric validation

Quantity must be a valid number greater than zero.

Unit price must be a valid number greater than or equal to zero.

VAT rate must be a valid number between 0 and 100, inclusive.

Invalid numeric values must be rejected.

4. Calculation rules

The application recalculates invoice amounts from the invoice lines.

Line total = quantity × unit price

Total excluding VAT = sum of line totals

VAT amount = total excluding VAT × VAT rate / 100

Total including VAT = total excluding VAT + VAT amount

Amounts are rounded to two decimal places.

5. Saving and persistence

The frontend validates the form before sending a save request. The backend must independently validate incoming data and recalculate totals before persisting the draft.

A successfully saved draft must be retrievable after refreshing or reopening the invoice correction page.
